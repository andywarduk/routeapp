Turn on HSTS in nginx.conf.template once https has been running cleanly for a
while. Left off deliberately for the cutover: a max-age of a year is not
practically reversible, since browsers that have seen it refuse plain http for
that long regardless of what the server later says. Raise it in stages -
max-age=300, then a day, then a year.
